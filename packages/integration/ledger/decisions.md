- 2026-11-24, `tail-ledger-clear` (p. 16): stop ledger-line 8th and 16th stems at the middle line rather than Gould's full outer-line clearance, and extend each extra tail (32nd, 64th, ...) past the middle line by its standard extra-flag increment (`¾` space per tail). Pushing Bravura's taller flag glyphs completely off the outer stave line produced awkwardly long stems extending past the far side of the stave.
- 2026-11-24, `stem-thickness` (p. 13): fixed by #3581 with Bravura's proportions (`1.2px` default stems against `1.3px` default stave lines). - 2026-11-23, `stem-middle-line` (pp. 13–14): rejected, fixed by #3582. A middle-line note follows the nearest non-middle-line notes either side within the bar when they agree or only one exists; when they disagree, the shared direction of its beat or half-bar; otherwise it stems down. The reason: Gould's examples stem a middle-line note with its neighbours, which vexml does not.
