### The rule

On a tied altered unison, draw each tie between its own noteheads (natural to natural, sharp to sharp). See Behind Bars p. 69.

### The test case

`ties-voices.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 11. Single-stemmed altered unison F4 natural + F#4 half notes, both tied to F4 natural + F#4.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, upper tie up and lower tie down between their own heads
- MuseScore: agrees, upper tie up and lower tie down between their own heads
- Verovio: agrees, upper tie up and lower tie down between their own heads
- OSMD: agrees, upper tie up and lower tie down between their own heads
