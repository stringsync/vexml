### The rule

With a tenuto line between a slur and a stem, centre the slur on the notehead like the tenuto line. See Behind Bars p. 122.

### The test case

`articulation-marks.musicxml` isolates each rule by measure.

- Measure 6. A slur above four stems-up notes with tenuto lines at the stem ends.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, slur centred on the head like the tenuto
- MuseScore: agrees, slur ends centred over the heads
- Verovio: agrees, slur ends centred over the heads
- OSMD: agrees, slur ends near the head centres

### Checks

1. Is the slur centred on the noteheads, like the tenuto lines?
   Crop: measure 6, 5 above, 8 below

### Why a person decides

Gould changes the centring with the kind of mark, and the tenuto line's own placement is open.
