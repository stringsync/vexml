### The rule

Preserve the layout of a second within a larger chord when forcing its outermost heads onto the ordinary side would reverse that second. (Behind Bars p. 49)

### The test case

`single-stem-chords.musicxml` places E5 and F5 above a separated B4 in measure 17.

### Checks

1. Is E5 left of F5 even though the highest note is displaced?
   Crop: measure 17, from 3 spaces, 7 spaces
