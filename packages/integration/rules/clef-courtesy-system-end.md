### The rule

When a new system begins in a different clef, a small cautionary clef appears at the end of the previous system before its closing barline (Behind Bars p. 7). Warning the player at the line end prevents misreading the first notes after the system break.

### The test case

`clefs.musicxml`: a five-line stave in 4/4 that ends system 1 in treble clef (measure 4) and forces system 2 onto a new system in bass clef (measure 5).

### What to look for

A blue vertical line marks the closing barline of system 1 (measure 4). Look between measure 4's last note and that barline: a small courtesy bass clef should appear there, rather than an empty gap.
