### The rule

A plain notehead is one stave-space tall: in a space it fills the gap between the two bounding lines, and on a line it centres directly on that line (Behind Bars p. 10). Matching notehead height to the stave-space keeps pitch positions unambiguous and prevents white noteheads from filling in.

### The test case

`noteheads.musicxml`: measures 1–3 on a treble stave in 4/4 with plain black quarters (F4, G4, A4, B4), white halves (F4, G4), and a whole note (F4), alternating space and line pitches with default sizing and placement.

### What to look for

Green vertical brackets to the left of each notehead in measures 1–3 show its height in quarter stave-spaces (all `1`); hover each bracket to see both height and centre offset from the nearest line (`½` for spaces, `0` for lines).
