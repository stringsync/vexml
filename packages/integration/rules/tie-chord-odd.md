### The rule

With an odd number of chord ties, curve the majority away from the stem. The stem side has less room. See Behind Bars p. 65.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 25. An up-stem chord, E4 G4 C5, tied.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, two of three away from the stem
- MuseScore: agrees, top up and lower two down
- Verovio: agrees, top up and lower two down
- OSMD: agrees, top up and lower two down

### Checks

1. Does the top tie curve up and the lower two down?
   Crop: measure 25, 3 above, 4 below
