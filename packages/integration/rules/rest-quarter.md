### The rule

A quarter rest starts in the top stave space and finishes in the bottom stave space (Behind Bars p. 35). Centring its vertical span across the middle three lines keeps it balanced inside the stave.

### The test case

`rests.musicxml`: measure 4 sets four quarter rests in 4/4 treble clef, measuring the first rest's top edge (expected within `0` to `1` spaces) and bottom edge (expected within `3` to `4` spaces) from the top line.

### What to look for

In measure 4, a green vertical bracket (`½..3½`) beside the first quarter rest between the blue top and bottom stave lines confirms it starts in the top space and ends in the bottom space.
