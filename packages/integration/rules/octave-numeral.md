### The rule

An octave sign's numeral is italic, 1½ stave-spaces high, and placed just left of the first note to which it applies (Behind Bars p. 28). Sizing the numeral clearly and leading the first note lets the player register the transposition before reading the pitch.

### The test case

`octave.musicxml`: measure 1 sets an `8va` above four accented quarter notes (C6 to F6), measuring the numeral's height in quarter stave-spaces and its left edge against the first notehead's left edge.

### What to look for

In measure 1, a green vertical bracket beside the `8` shows its `1½`-space height, and a green box (`left`) beside the blue reference line on the first notehead's left edge shows the numeral starts just left of the note.
