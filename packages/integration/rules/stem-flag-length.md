### The rule

Standard 3½-space stems accommodate one or two tails (eighth and sixteenth notes); each additional tail (32nd, 64th, and beyond) must lengthen the stem further (Behind Bars pp. 15–16). Lengthening only when extra tails require space keeps shorter subdivisions compact and prevents tails from crowding the notehead.

### The test case

`stems.musicxml`: measure 7 writes G4 as an eighth, sixteenth, 32nd, and 64th note followed by rests, with `<stem>` unset.

### What to look for

In measure 7, brackets beside the four flagged G4 stems show each length in quarter stave-spaces: the eighth and sixteenth measure `3½`, the 32nd lengthens to `4¼`, and the 64th lengthens to `5` (all green).
