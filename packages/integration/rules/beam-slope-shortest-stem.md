### The rule

In a slanted beamed group, the shortest stem sets the beam's anchor and may shorten to 3 stave-spaces inside the stave or 2½ stave-spaces outside the stave, never shorter (Behind Bars p. 21). Allowing slight shortening on the closest note keeps the beam angle moderate without cramping the notehead against the beam.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measures 10 and 13 hold slanted beamed groups inside the stave and measure 14 holds beamed pairs outside the stave on ledger lines, measuring each group's shortest stem from notehead centre to the outer beam edge.

### What to look for

In measures 10, 13, and 14, green brackets beside each group's shortest stem confirm every inside-stave stem stays at least `3` spaces and every outside-stave stem stays at least `2½` spaces.
