### The rule

When alternating flute overtones of a fundamental, write the fingered fundamental as a diamond notehead below the ordinary sounding pitch on the same stem, with a small open harmonic circle above the sounding pitch. See Behind Bars pp. 256–257.

### The test case

`woodwind.musicxml` measure 3 writes two half-note flute overtone chords pairing a lower diamond fundamental with an upper sounding-pitch harmonic.

- Measure 3. Beat 1: C4 diamond + C6 sounding-pitch harmonic. Beat 3: D4 diamond + A5 sounding-pitch harmonic.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, draws the diamond fundamental below the ordinary sounding pitch on a shared stem with the small harmonic circle above
- MuseScore: agrees, draws the lower diamond fundamental and upper ordinary head on a shared down-stem with the harmonic circle above
- Verovio: agrees, draws the lower diamond fundamental and upper ordinary head on a shared down-stem with the harmonic circle above
- OSMD: agrees, draws the lower diamond fundamental and upper ordinary head on a shared down-stem with the harmonic circle above
