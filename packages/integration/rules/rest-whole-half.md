### The rule

A whole rest hangs from the second stave line down and a half rest sits on the middle stave line (Behind Bars p. 34). Anchoring each block to its own line lets the player tell whole and half rests apart at a glance.

### The test case

`rests.musicxml`: measure 1 sets a single whole rest and measure 2 sets two half rests in 4/4 treble clef, measuring the whole rest's top edge and the first half rest's bottom edge in quarter stave-spaces from the top line.

### What to look for

In measure 1, a red box (`top 2`) shows the whole rest hanging from the middle line instead of the blue reference line on the second stave line (`top 1`). In measure 2, a green box (`bot 2`) shows the half rest sitting cleanly on the middle line.
