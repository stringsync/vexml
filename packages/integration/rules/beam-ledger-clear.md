### The rule

For beamed notes on ledger lines, at least one clear stave line must separate the innermost beam from the first ledger line (Behind Bars p. 19). Keeping the outer stave line free of beams prevents ledger lines and beams from visually merging.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 9 holds three beamed ledger-line groups (C6 eighths, C6 sixteenths, and A3 sixteenths), checking that each group's innermost beam stays clear of the top or bottom outer stave line.

### What to look for

In measure 9, blue reference lines mark the top and bottom outer stave lines and green vertical brackets labeled `clear` show the gap between the outer stave line and each group's innermost beam.
