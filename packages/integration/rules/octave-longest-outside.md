### The rule

When octave signs appear alongside phrase marks or tuplet brackets, whichever spans the longer duration goes on the outside (Behind Bars p. 29). Nesting shorter spans inside longer ones keeps the hierarchy of brackets clear.

### The test case

`octave.musicxml`: measure 7 sets a single `8va` spanning two beamed eighth-note triplets (each with a tuplet bracket) followed by a half note, checking that both tuplet brackets remain inside the octave sign.

### What to look for

In measure 7, a blue reference box marks the `8va` above the stave and green boxes (`inside`) mark both tuplet brackets cleanly below the octave line.
