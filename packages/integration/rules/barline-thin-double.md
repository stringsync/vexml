### The rule

Mark a section division with two ordinary-weight strokes about three-quarters of a stave space apart. (Behind Bars p. 39)

### The test case

`barlines.musicxml` uses a thin double divider after four quarters in measure 2.

### Checks

1. Do both strokes have the same weight as the ordinary divider?
   Crop: measure 1, from 26 spaces, 21 spaces

2. Are the two strokes about three-quarters of a space apart?
   Crop: measure 2, from 15 spaces, 5 spaces
