### The rule

A lone bar-length note in a wide bar can sit closer to the opening divider to avoid looking isolated. (Behind Bars p. 41)

### The test case

`barline-score.musicxml` uses two simultaneous whole notes in a wide opening measure.

### Checks

1. Do the whole notes feel connected to the opening signatures in the wide first bar?
   Crop: measure 1

### Why a person decides

The reference allows a choice based on the visual balance of the wide bar.
