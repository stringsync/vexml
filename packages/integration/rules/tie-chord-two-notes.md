### The rule

Curve the two ties of a two-note chord in opposite directions. Each tie sits on the open side of its head. See Behind Bars p. 65.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 24. F4 and A4 half notes tied.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, upper up and lower down
- MuseScore: agrees, upper up and lower down
- Verovio: agrees, upper up and lower down
- OSMD: agrees, upper up and lower down

### Checks

1. Does the upper tie curve up and the lower one down?
   Crop: measure 24, 3 above, 4 below
