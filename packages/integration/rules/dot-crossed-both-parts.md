# dot-crossed-both-parts

### The rule

Dotted crossed parts can share a dot column to the right of both heads in cramped settings. See Behind Bars p. 57.

### The test case

`duration-dots.musicxml` uses one attack per measure to isolate the dot placement.

- Measure 29. Crossed dotted parts a third apart.
- Measure 38. Crossed dotted parts a fourth apart.

### Checks

1. Can each dot be matched to its voice in this crossed pair?
   Crop: measure 29, 10 spaces, 3 above, 3 below
2. Can each dot be matched to its voice in this crossed pair a fourth apart?
   Crop: measure 38, 7 spaces, 3 above, 3 below

### Why a person decides

The book permits the compact layout and leaves the choice to the space available.
