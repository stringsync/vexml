### The rule

When notes are added or released simultaneously above and below the tied notes of a single-stemmed chord, place ties in their conventional directions. See Behind Bars p. 71.

### The test case

`ties-voices.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 19. A4 C5 half tied to F4 A4 C5 E5 half, adding notes both above and below simultaneously.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, C5 tie curves up and A4 tie curves down
- MuseScore: agrees, C5 tie curves up and A4 tie curves down
- Verovio: agrees, C5 tie curves up and A4 tie curves down
- OSMD: agrees, C5 tie curves up and A4 tie curves down

   Crop: measure 19, 4 above, 4 below
