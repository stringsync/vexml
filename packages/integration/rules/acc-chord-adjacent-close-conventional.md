### The rule

When a close-position chord has several accidentals including adjacent notes, use the conventional alternating arrangement (outermost closest to the chord) rather than descending order, which takes too much horizontal space. See Behind Bars p. 90.

### The test case

`accidentals-voices.musicxml` isolates each case in its own measure.

- Measure 3. Two close-position four-note half-note chords: F#4, G#4, A#4, C#5 and G♮4, A♭4, B♭4, D♭5.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, uses conventional alternating order rather than descending order
- MuseScore: agrees, alternates from outer accidentals inward on both close-position chords
- Verovio: agrees, alternates from outer accidentals inward on both close-position chords
- OSMD: agrees, alternates from outer accidentals inward on both close-position chords
