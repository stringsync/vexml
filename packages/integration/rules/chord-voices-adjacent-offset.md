### The rule

When a tone or semitone separates the voices, shift the lower voice right and align the upper voice with an accompanying part. (Behind Bars pp. 49, 53)

### The test case

`double-stem-chords.musicxml` places B4 and C5 at the boundary between two simultaneous adjacent-note chords.

### Checks

1. Is the lower chord displaced right of the upper chord?
   Crop: measure 2, 7 spaces

2. Does the upper chord stay at the accompaniment’s horizontal onset?
   Crop: measure 2, part 1, 7 spaces, 3 above, 11 below
