### The rule

Use separate up-stems for the upper voice and down-stems for the lower voice, even when their rhythms differ or pitches cross. (Behind Bars pp. 47, 52)

### The test case

`double-stem-chords.musicxml` pairs different lengths in measure 3 and crosses voice pitches in measures 11 and 12.

### Checks

1. Do the separate half and quarter notes have opposing stems?
   Crop: measure 3, 7 spaces, 6 above

2. Does the lower-pitched upper voice still point up where the voices cross?
   Crop: measure 11, 7 spaces

3. Do the overlapping chords retain stems for their respective voices?
   Crop: measure 12, 7 spaces, 6 above
