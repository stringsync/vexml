### The rule

When two voices share a head at a unison, keep both opposing stems so the independent voices remain evident. (Behind Bars p. 52)

### The test case

`double-stem-chords.musicxml` uses two voices with D5 half notes on the same beat.

### Checks

1. Are an up-stem and a down-stem both visible at the unison?
   Crop: measure 4, 7 spaces
