### The rule

Leave about half a stave-space between a stem end and articulation outside the stave. See Behind Bars p. 121.

### The test case

`articulation.musicxml` isolates each rule by measure.

- Measure 2. Two parts whose stems end outside the stave, each note with staccato.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, about half a space past the stem end
- MuseScore: agrees, about half a space past the stem ends
- Verovio: differs, dots almost touch the stem ends
- OSMD: agrees, half to one space past the stem ends

### Checks

1. Is each dot about half a space past its stem end?
