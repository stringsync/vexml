### The rule

An octave sign applying to a single note takes an end corner after the `8` so it is immediately clear that only that note is transposed (Behind Bars p. 31). Without a closing hook, a lone numeral can look unfinished or ambiguous.

### The test case

`octave.musicxml`: measure 3 sets an `8va` over a single quarter note C6 followed by three untransposed G4 quarter notes, checking for a closing end corner on the bracket's right edge.

### What to look for

In measure 3, a green box at the right edge of the single-note `8va` confirms the closing end corner after the numeral.
