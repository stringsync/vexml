### The rule

Always place a mid-system time-signature change after the barline dividing the old and new metres. See Behind Bars p. 152.

### The test case

`metre-time.musicxml` changes from 3/4 to 12/16 mid-system at measure 3.

- Measure 3. Mid-system time-signature change to 12/16.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, places the new time signature after the barline
- MuseScore: agrees, places 12/16 to the right of the barline opening measure 3
- Verovio: agrees, places 12/16 to the right of the barline opening measure 3
- OSMD: agrees, places 12/16 to the right of the barline opening measure 3

### Checks

1. Does the 12/16 time signature sit to the right of the barline opening measure 3?
   Crop: measure 3, 6 spaces, 2 above, 2 below
