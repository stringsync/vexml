### The rule

Flatten a long tie so its curve is not much deeper than a short one’s. Ties of very different heights look unrelated. See Behind Bars p. 61.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measures 6 and 7. An A4 whole note tied over the barline.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, long ties barely deeper than short ones
- MuseScore: agrees, a long tie only a little deeper than a short one
- Verovio: agrees, a long, flat tie
- OSMD: agrees, a long, flat tie

### Checks

1. Is the long tie about as deep as the short ties in measure 1?
   Crop: measure 6, 3 above, 4 below
