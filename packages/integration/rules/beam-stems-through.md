### The rule

Every stem in a beamed group should pass through all inner beams and connect solidly to the outer beam (Behind Bars p. 17). Stopping inner stems short of the outer beam breaks the visual unity of the group.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: all beamed stems across measures 1–23, checking that each stem tip meets its group's outer beam band without stopping short or protruding past it.

### What to look for

In measures 1 and 2, blue lines mark the outer beam edges and green boxes labeled `through` confirm that all stems pass through the inner beams to the outer beam (any stem stopping short across the score would be boxed in red).
