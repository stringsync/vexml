### The rule

Both ends of a slanted beam inside the stave should attach to a stave line rather than starting or ending in the middle of a stave-space (Behind Bars pp. 20–21). Ending a slanted beam mid-space creates a narrow white wedge between the beam and the adjacent stave line that tends to fill in visually.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 13 holds four rising beamed groups inside the stave (E4 to G4 and F4 to A4 eighths, G4 to C5 sixteenths, and E4 to A4 32nds), checking every beam band at the first and last stem of each group for mid-space ends inside the stave.

### What to look for

In measure 13, boxes at the start and end stems of each beamed group mark line-attached ends in green (`line`) and flag the starting stems of the sixteenth and 32nd groups in red (`1 mid`) where a beam band ends mid-space.
