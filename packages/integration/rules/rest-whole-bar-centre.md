### The rule

Place a whole-bar semibreve rest at the visual centre of the bar (or immediately to the left of a central note), even when the rhythmic midpoint in another part lies far off-centre. See Behind Bars p. 159.

### The test case

`metre-rests.musicxml` measures 3 and 4 place a whole-bar rest in staff 2 under two contrasting rhythms in staff 1.

- Measure 3. Staff 1 has four evenly spaced quarter notes; staff 2 has a whole-bar rest.
- Measure 4. Staff 1 has twelve 16th notes followed by a quarter note so the rhythmic midpoint sits far to the right; staff 2 has a whole-bar rest.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, places the whole-bar rest at the visual centre of the bar even when the other part's rhythmic midpoint is off-centre
- MuseScore: agrees, centres the whole-bar rest visually in both measures 3 and 4
- Verovio: agrees, centres the whole-bar rest visually in both measures 3 and 4
- OSMD: agrees, centres the whole-bar rest visually in both measures 3 and 4

### Checks

1. In measure 3, does the whole-bar rest sit near the visual centre of the bar?

2. In measure 4, does the whole-bar rest stay at the visual centre of the bar rather than shifting right toward the rhythmic midpoint?
