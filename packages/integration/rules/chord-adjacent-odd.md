### The rule

Alternate an odd run of adjacent heads with most on the ordinary stem side, placing the lowest head left for up-stems and right for down-stems. (Behind Bars p. 49)

### The test case

`single-stem-chords.musicxml` pairs a high three-note run with a low three-note run.

### Checks

1. Does the down-stemmed run alternate right, left, right from the bottom?
   Crop: measure 8, 8 spaces

2. Does the up-stemmed run alternate left, right, left from the bottom?
   Crop: measure 8, from 8 spaces, 12 spaces
