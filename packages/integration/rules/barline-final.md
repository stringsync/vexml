### The rule

Close a movement with a thin stroke about half a stave space before a beam-weight stroke. (Behind Bars p. 39)

### The test case

`barlines.musicxml` uses a closing pair of thin and thick strokes and a single joining beam for comparison.

### Checks

1. Is the heavy closing stroke about as thick as the beam?
   Crop: measure 5, from 10 spaces, 5 spaces

2. Is the clear gap from the thin stroke to the thick one about half a space?
   Crop: measure 5, from 11 spaces, 4 spaces

3. Is the thin stroke as heavy and as solid as an ordinary barline?
   Crop: measure 5, from 10 spaces, 5 spaces
