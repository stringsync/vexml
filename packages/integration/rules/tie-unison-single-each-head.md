### The rule

On a single-stemmed perfect unison, each notehead takes its own tie, with the upper tie for the left notehead and the lower tie for the right notehead. See Behind Bars p. 69.

### The test case

`ties-voices.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 9. Single-stemmed half-note perfect unison on G4, both heads tied to G4 unison.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, upper tie up and lower tie down, one per head
- MuseScore: agrees, upper tie up and lower tie down, one per head
- Verovio: agrees, upper tie up and lower tie down, one per head
- OSMD: agrees, upper tie up and lower tie down, one per head
