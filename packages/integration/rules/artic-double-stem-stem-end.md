### The rule

In double-stemmed writing, put each part's articulation at its stem end, never on the notehead side and no closer than the stem end. See Behind Bars p. 117–118.

### The test case

`articulation.musicxml` isolates each rule by measure.

- Measure 2. Two parts: an up-stemmed upper part and a down-stemmed lower part, each note with staccato.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, marks at the stem ends
- MuseScore: agrees, dots beyond both stem ends
- Verovio: agrees, dots just beyond the stem ends
- OSMD: agrees, dots beyond both stem ends
