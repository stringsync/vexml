### The rule

Place the strong accent above the stave whatever the stem direction. See Behind Bars p. 117.

### The test case

`articulation-marks.musicxml` isolates each rule by measure.

- Measure 1. A strong accent on two down-stemmed and two up-stemmed notes.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, strong accent above the stave for every stem
- MuseScore: agrees, all four above the stave
- Verovio: agrees, all four above the stave
- OSMD: differs, up-stem accents drawn below, inverted

### Checks

1. Is each strong accent above the stave, including those on the up-stems?
   Crop: measure 1, 5 above, 6 below
