### The rule

Centre an accidental exactly on its note's line or space, taking particular care with ledger notes, where no stave lines guide the eye and a slight shift reads as another pitch. See Behind Bars p. 78.

### The test case

`accidentals.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 3. C#6 on the second ledger line above, then A#3 on the second ledger line below, each with a sharp.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, states it in prose with no example cropped
- MuseScore: agrees, both sharps centred on their ledger lines
- Verovio: agrees, both sharps centred on their ledger lines
- OSMD: agrees, both sharps centred on their ledger lines
