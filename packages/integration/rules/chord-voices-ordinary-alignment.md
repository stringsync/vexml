### The rule

Align the undisplaced heads of simultaneous adjacent-note chords in different voices to make their common onset clear. (Behind Bars p. 49)

### The test case

`double-stem-chords.musicxml` puts a second in each voice of the upper stave, with space between the voices.

### Checks

1. Do the heads on the ordinary stem sides of both voices align?
   Crop: measure 1, from 7 spaces, 7 spaces
