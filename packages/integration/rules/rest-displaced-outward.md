### The rule

When a note in the other part occupies a rest's normal two-voice position, the rest moves further away from the stave so it clears the note cleanly (Behind Bars p. 37). Moving outward prevents the rest from colliding with the active voice's notehead or stem.

### The test case

`rests-voices.musicxml`: measure 5 places a V1 quarter rest directly above a V2 C5 quarter note on beat 1, checking that the rest's bottom edge clears the top of the C5 notehead.

### What to look for

In measure 5, a green box (`clear`) around V1's raised quarter rest sits cleanly above the blue reference line marking the top of V2's C5 notehead.
