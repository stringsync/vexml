### The rule

Within a passage of single-stemmed moving chords, chords tied without any change of pitch revert to their conventional tie directions. See Behind Bars p. 71.

### The test case

`ties-voices.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 22. G4 B4 quarter moves to B4 D5 quarter (B4 tied), which then ties unchanged to B4 D5 half.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, unchanging B4 D5 chord curves D5 up and B4 down
- MuseScore: agrees, unchanging B4 D5 chord curves D5 up and B4 down
- Verovio: agrees, unchanging B4 D5 chord curves D5 up and B4 down
- OSMD: agrees, unchanging B4 D5 chord curves D5 up and B4 down

### Checks

1. Do the two ties on the unchanging B4 D5 chord curve in conventional opposite directions (D5 up, B4 down)?
