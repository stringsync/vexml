### The rule

A beam should slant in the direction of the interval between its outer notes, regardless of the direction of the inner notes (Behind Bars p. 22). Following the outer contour gives a clear, consistent visual cue for the group's overall motion without being pulled astray by inner leaps.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 15 holds two four-eighth multi-directional groups (C5, E4, F4, G4 where the outer interval falls from C5 to G4, and G4, C4, E4, A4 where the outer interval rises from G4 to A4), checking the slope direction of each beam.

### What to look for

In measure 15, red boxes on both beams report `flat` where the first group should fall (`falls`) and the second group should rise (`rises`).
