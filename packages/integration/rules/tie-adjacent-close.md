### The rule

In a chord with a second, start each tie close to its own head rather than after both. Ties close to their heads show which note each holds. See Behind Bars p. 63.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 13. A4 and B4 half-note second tied to a quarter.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, each tie starts at its own head
- MuseScore: agrees, each tie starts at its own head
- Verovio: agrees, each tie starts at its own head
- OSMD: agrees, each tie starts at its own head
