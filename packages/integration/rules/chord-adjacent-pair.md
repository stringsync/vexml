### The rule

Write an isolated second with the lower head left and the upper head right, even when the shared pitch changes sides on successive chords. (Behind Bars pp. 48–49)

### The test case

`single-stem-chords.musicxml` uses G4 as the upper head in the first second and the lower head in the next one.

### Checks

1. Does the first pair rise to the right?
   Crop: measure 7, 8 spaces

2. Does the next pair rise to the right with G4 now on the left?
   Crop: measure 7, from 8 spaces, 12 spaces
