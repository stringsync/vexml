### The rule

Ledger lines are spaced the same distance apart as stave lines, but drawn visibly thicker, around twice the thickness of a stave line (Behind Bars p. 26). Distinctly heavier ledger lines let a player count how many lines sit above or below the stave at a glance.

### The test case

`ledgers.musicxml`: measure 1 holds four quarter notes on ledger lines above and below a treble stave (C6, D6, A3, C4), comparing the vertical thickness of each ledger line against the stave's own line thickness.

### What to look for

In measure 1, green boxes mark every ledger line on C6, D6, A3, and C4, each reporting `2.0×` the stave-line thickness (expected at least `1.6×`).
