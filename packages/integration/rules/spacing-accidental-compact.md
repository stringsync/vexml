### The rule

Keep accidentals close to their own heads or adjoining accidentals as far as the shapes allow without touching. (Behind Bars p. 42)

### The test case

`symbol-spacing.musicxml` uses a sharp and a flat at different heights in the opening chord.

### Checks

1. Do the accidentals read as part of the chord while staying clear of one another and the heads?
   Crop: measure 6, from 10 spaces, 8 spaces, 6 above

### Why a person decides

How tightly the two glyph shapes can nest without looking joined depends on their contours and vertical overlap.
