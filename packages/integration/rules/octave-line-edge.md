### The rule

The dashed extension line of an octave sign runs from the top edge of the `8` when placed above the stave (`8va`) and from the base of the `8` when placed below (`8vb`) (Behind Bars p. 28). Aligning the line with the outer edge frames the transposed passage cleanly without cutting across the numeral.

### The test case

`octave.musicxml`: measure 1 checks an `8va` above the treble stave against the numeral's top edge, and measure 4 checks an `8vb` below the stave against the numeral's bottom edge.

### What to look for

In measure 1, a green box (`0`) beside the blue top-edge reference line shows the `8va` dashes run from the top of the `8`. In measure 4, a green box (`0`) beside the base reference line shows the `8vb` dashes align with the base of the `8`.
