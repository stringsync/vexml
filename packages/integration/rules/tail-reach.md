### The rule

An up-stem's tail should finish opposite or just above the notehead without dipping past it, whereas a down-stem's tail may curve as far as to touch the notehead without cutting into it (Behind Bars p. 15).

### The test case

`tails.musicxml`: measure 1 places an up-stemmed G4 eighth and a down-stemmed C5 eighth separated by rests, with default stem and flag placement.

### What to look for

In measure 1, blue lines mark each notehead's bottom edge and green boxes outline the tail ink beside each stem: `beside the head` on the up-stem G4 and `clear or touching` on the down-stem C5.
