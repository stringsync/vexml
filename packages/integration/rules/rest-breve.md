### The rule

A breve rest fills the entire stave space between the second line down and the middle line (Behind Bars p. 35). Filling that full space distinguishes a double-whole rest from the half-space whole and half rests.

### The test case

`rests.musicxml`: measure 3 sets a breve rest in 8/4 treble clef, measuring its top and bottom edges in quarter stave-spaces from the top line against the second and middle lines (`1` to `2`).

### What to look for

In measure 3, a red vertical bracket (`½..3½`) beside the rest shows a quarter-rest glyph drawn instead of a breve rest filling the space between the two blue reference lines.
