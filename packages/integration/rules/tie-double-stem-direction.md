### The rule

In double-stemmed writing, curve the upper part’s ties up and the lower part’s ties down. Curving each tie towards its own stem keeps the two voices visually separate. See Behind Bars p. 67.

### The test case

`ties-voices.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 1. Voice 1 ties C5 half to C5 half; voice 2 ties F4 half to F4 half.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, C5 tie curves up, F4 tie curves down
- MuseScore: agrees, C5 tie curves up, F4 tie curves down
- Verovio: agrees, C5 tie curves up, F4 tie curves down
- OSMD: agrees, C5 tie curves up, F4 tie curves down

### Checks

1. Does the upper voice’s C5 tie curve up and the lower voice’s F4 tie curve down?
   Crop: measure 1, 4 above, 4 below
