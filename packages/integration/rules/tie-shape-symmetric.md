### The rule

Draw a tie as a tapered arc, symmetrical about its middle, between two heads of the same pitch. A symmetrical, tapered arc reads as a tie rather than a slur or a line. See Behind Bars p. 60.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 1. Two A4 half notes tied.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, a tapered arc deepest at its middle
- MuseScore: agrees, a tapered, even arc
- Verovio: agrees, a short tapered, even arc
- OSMD: agrees, a thin, even arc

### Checks

1. Does the tie bow evenly, with its deepest point halfway along and thin tips?
   Crop: measure 1, 3 above, 4 below
