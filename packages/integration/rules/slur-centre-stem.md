### The rule

Centre a slur on the stems at its ends when it sits at the stem ends. See Behind Bars p. 122.

### The test case

`slurs-structure.musicxml` isolates each rule by measure.

- Measure 5. A slur placed above four stems-up notes.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, centres each slur end on its stem
- MuseScore: agrees, ends the slur over the first and last stems
- Verovio: agrees, ends the slur over the first and last stems
- OSMD: agrees, ends the slur over the first and last stems

### Checks

1. Does the slur start and end over the first and last stems?
