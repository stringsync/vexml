### The rule

When all notes in a beamed group lie beyond the first ledger line, the beam takes only a slight slope: ¼ stave-space for an interval of a second and ½ stave-space for any wider interval (Behind Bars p. 21). Keeping ledger-line beams gently angled preserves clear stem proportions without steep plunges across the stave.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 14 holds four ledger-line eighth pairs: above the stave on C6 to E6 (third) and C6 to D6 (second), and below the stave on A3 to F3 (third) and A3 to G3 (second), measuring each beam's vertical rise or fall in quarter-spaces.

### What to look for

In measure 14, red boxes on all four ledger-line beams report a slope of `0` (horizontal) where the thirds should slope `½` space and the seconds `¼` space.
