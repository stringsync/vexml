### The rule

Each ledger line extends slightly past both sides of its notehead, while ledger lines on neighbouring notes remain separate rather than joining into a continuous bar (Behind Bars p. 26). Clear overhangs anchor the pitch on the line, and visible gaps between adjacent ledgers keep dense passages legible.

### The test case

`ledgers.musicxml`: measure 1 checks four ledger-line quarter notes (C6, D6, A3, C4) for overhang on both sides of the notehead, and measure 2 sets eight beamed C6 sixteenth notes to confirm neighbouring ledger lines stay apart.

### What to look for

In measure 1, green brackets above each note's ledger lines show `+3/+3px` overhang on both sides of the notehead. In measure 2, a green box labelled `apart` spans the eight sixteenth notes' ledgers with zero joined segments.
