### The rule

When four beams sit within the stave, they ideally spread slightly to attach to stave lines, or otherwise leave at most one beam floating in a space as an acceptable compromise (Behind Bars p. 18). Allowing at most one floating beam avoids excessive stem lengthening while keeping the stave readable.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 8 holds two four-beam (64th-note) groups on G4 (up-stems) and C5 (down-stems), counting how many beam bands float mid-space inside the stave in each group.

### What to look for

In measure 8, green boxes around both 64th-note beam stacks read `0 mid-space`, confirming neither group exceeds the allowance of at most one floating beam.
