### The rule

In a double-stemmed altered unison, separate the noteheads and place each accidental beside its own part so it is unmistakable which accidental belongs to which voice (for example, never placing a cancelling natural before a tied sharp). See Behind Bars p. 91.

### The test case

`accidentals-voices.musicxml` isolates each case in its own measure.

- Measures 7–8. Voice 2 holds a tied F#4 into measure 8 while voice 1 enters on F♮4; beat 3 of measure 8 pairs voice 1 C#5 with voice 2 C♮5.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, separates the altered-unison heads and places each accidental beside its own part
- MuseScore: differs, separates the heads on both beats but places the accidentals grouped before both heads
- Verovio: agrees, separates the heads and places the second accidental between the heads on beat 3 (before both on the tied beat 1)
- OSMD: differs, merges each altered unison into a single shared notehead with accidentals grouped before it
