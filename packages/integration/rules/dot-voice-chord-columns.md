# dot-voice-chord-columns

### The rule

Arrange the dots of each adjacent-note chord in its own vertical column in two-voice music. See Behind Bars p. 57.

### The test case

`duration-dots.musicxml` uses one attack per measure to isolate the dot placement.

- Measure 16. Two dotted chords with seconds.

### Checks

1. Does each voice have a column of two dots after its own chord?
   Crop: measure 16, 7 spaces, 3 above, 3 below
