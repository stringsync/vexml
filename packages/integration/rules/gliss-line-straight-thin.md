### The rule

A glissando is drawn as a thin straight diagonal line. See Behind Bars p. 140.

### The test case

`glissandos.musicxml` isolates glissando lines by measure.

- Measure 1. A half-note `C4` sliding up to a half-note `G4`.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, thin straight diagonal line
- MuseScore: differs, draws a wavy line
- Verovio: agrees, straight diagonal line, slightly heavier
- OSMD: agrees, thin straight diagonal line

### Checks

1. In measure 1, is the glissando a thin straight diagonal line?
   Crop: measure 1, 4 above, 6 below
