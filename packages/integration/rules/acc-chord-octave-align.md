### The rule

Align the accidentals of two chord notes an octave or more apart in one column, which keeps the group compact. See Behind Bars p. 88.

### The test case

`accidentals.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 9. F#4, F#5 half-note octave, then E♭4, A♭5 half-note eleventh.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, states it in prose with no example on the page
- MuseScore: agrees, both pairs aligned
- Verovio: agrees, both pairs aligned
- OSMD: agrees, both pairs aligned
