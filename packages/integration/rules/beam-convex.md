### The rule

A beamed group with a convex shape (where the inner notes lie further from the beam than the outer notes) should take a sloping beam in the direction of its outer interval (Behind Bars p. 23). Because no inner note crowds the beam, the slope can follow the outer contour cleanly.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 17's second group holds three up-stem eighths on G4, D4, A4 (inner D4 is further from the beam than outer G4 and A4), checking that the beam rises from G4 to A4.

### What to look for

In measure 17, a red box on the second group's beam reports `flat` where it should slope upward (`rises`).
