### The rule

When two stemmed voices occupy ledger lines at the same beat, they may share ledger lines spanning the noteheads between the stave and the displaced voice, and unshared ledger lines must not cut through the stem of the voice closer to the stave (Behind Bars p. 27). Cleanly shared ledgers prevent clutter and keep each voice's stem unobstructed.

### The test case

`ledgers.musicxml`: measure 5 sets two half notes in voice 1 (E6, E6, stemmed up) over two half notes in voice 2 (A5, C6, stemmed down) above a treble stave.

### What to look for

Judged by eye, because the ink masks cannot attribute a shared ledger line to one voice over another. In measure 5, verify that the A5 ledger on beat 1 and the A5 and C6 ledgers on beat 2 are cleanly shared by both voices and that no ledger line cuts through voice 2's downward stems.
