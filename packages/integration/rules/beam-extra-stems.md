### The rule

When three or more beams are stacked, the outer beam extends further away from the notehead so that no inner beam comes closer than 2½ stave-spaces to the notehead centre (Behind Bars p. 19). Extending the stem outward preserves clear space between noteheads and dense beam stacks.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: all three- and four-beam groups across measures 2 and 4–8 (G4, A4, C5, and D5 in 32nds and 64ths), measuring the distance from each notehead centre to the nearest edge of the innermost beam.

### What to look for

In measures 2 and 4–8, green vertical brackets on the first stem of every 32nd- and 64th-note group report `3` spaces from notehead centre to innermost beam, clearing the 2½-space minimum.
