### The rule

A horizontal beam inside the stave must hang from, sit on, or centre on a stave line rather than float in the middle of a space (Behind Bars pp. 17–18). Attaching horizontal beams to stave lines prevents narrow white slivers that obscure both the beam and the stave grid.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measures 4–7 hold repeated A4, G4, C5, and D5 groups in eighths, sixteenths, and 32nds (space and line notes, up- and down-stems), checking that no horizontal beam band inside the stave floats clear of every stave line.

### What to look for

In measures 4–7, green boxes labeled `on line` enclose each horizontal beam group, confirming that every beam band within the stave touches or straddles a stave line.
