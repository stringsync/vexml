### The rule

In two-part writing, whole and half rests must never stray across the middle stave line into the other voice's half of the stave (Behind Bars p. 36). Crossing the centre line causes the player to misread which voice is resting.

### The test case

`rests-voices.musicxml`: measure 2 sets an upper-voice half rest on beat 1 and a lower-voice half rest on beat 3, checking that V1's half rest stays at or above the middle line and V2's stays at or below it.

### What to look for

In measure 2, green boxes over V1's half rest (`V1 above`) and V2's half rest (`V2 below`) confirm neither crosses the blue middle stave line.
