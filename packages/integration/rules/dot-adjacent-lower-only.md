# dot-adjacent-lower-only

### The rule

In ordinary adjacent two-voice writing, keep the lone lower-voice dot on its own side of the other head. See Behind Bars p. 56.

### The test case

`duration-dots.musicxml` uses one attack per measure to isolate the dot placement.

- Measure 15. Only lower adjacent part dotted.

### Checks

1. Is the dot in the lower space and immediately right of the lower head?
   Crop: measure 15, 7 spaces, 3 above, 3 below
