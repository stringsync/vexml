### The rule

Centre articulation in line with its notehead. See Behind Bars p. 118.

### The test case

`articulation.musicxml` isolates each rule by measure.

- Measure 1. Staccato on two down-stemmed and two up-stemmed single notes.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, centred in line with the notehead
- MuseScore: agrees, dot centred over the head
- Verovio: agrees, dots centred over the heads
- OSMD: agrees, dot centred over the head
