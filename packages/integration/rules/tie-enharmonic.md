### The rule

Two enharmonically equal pitches may be tied. The sound is held even when the spelling changes. See Behind Bars p. 60.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 4. F sharp 4 tied to G flat 4.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, ties F sharp to G flat
- MuseScore: agrees, one tie from F sharp to G flat
- Verovio: agrees, one tie from F sharp to G flat
- OSMD: agrees, one tie from F sharp to G flat

### Checks
