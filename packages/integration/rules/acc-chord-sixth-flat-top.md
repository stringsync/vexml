### The rule

Align two accidentals a sixth apart when the upper one is a flat, whose bowl leaves room for the sign below. See Behind Bars p. 88.

### The test case

`accidentals.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 11. F#4, D♭5 half-note sixth, then E♭4, C♭5 half-note sixth.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, aligned under the upper flat (or offset)
- MuseScore: agrees, both pairs aligned
- Verovio: agrees, both pairs aligned
- OSMD: agrees, both pairs aligned

### Checks

1. Do both chords' accidentals line up under their upper flats?
