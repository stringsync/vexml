### The rule

In a chord with adjacent altered notes (seconds), accidentals may be arranged in descending order right to left away from the chord to reflect the notes, or in conventional outer-closest order. See Behind Bars p. 90.

### The test case

`accidentals-voices.musicxml` isolates each case in its own measure.

- Measure 2. Two half-note chords with seconds: F#4, G#4, D#5 and E♭4, F♭4, B♭4, C♭5.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, recommends descending order right to left for adjacent pairs while calling conventional outer-closest order acceptable
- MuseScore: agrees, uses conventional outer-closest order (descending on the first chord, outer-closest on the second)
- Verovio: agrees, uses conventional outer-closest order on both chords
- OSMD: agrees, uses conventional outer-closest order on both chords

### Why a person decides

Behind Bars explicitly permits both descending order right to left (recommended for pairs of seconds) and the conventional outer-closest chord accidental order.
