### The rule

A rest placed outside the stave should sit at roughly the same vertical level as its surrounding pitches, keeping its distance from the stave proportional to the melody it belongs to (Behind Bars p. 37). Matching the surrounding pitch level keeps the voice's horizontal reading line continuous.

### The test case

`rests-voices.musicxml`: measure 7 places a V1 half rest above a V2 A5 half note, followed immediately by a V1 C6 half note on beat 3, showing the raised rest beside its neighbouring pitch in the same voice.

### What to look for

Compare the height of V1's raised half rest on beat 1 of measure 7 against V1's following C6 half note on beat 3: the rest sits just above A5 and roughly level with C6 rather than floating far above or colliding with the lower voice. Because "level with" has no fixed numeric threshold, this rule is judged by eye.
