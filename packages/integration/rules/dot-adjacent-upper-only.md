# dot-adjacent-upper-only

### The rule

Keep a lone upper-voice dot beside its head. It may sit over the lower head or move outward to avoid resembling an articulation. See Behind Bars pp. 56–57.

### The test case

`duration-dots.musicxml` uses one attack per measure to isolate the dot placement.

- Measure 14. Only upper adjacent part dotted.

### Checks

1. Does the upper dot read with its note without resembling a staccato on the lower note?
   Crop: measure 14, 7 spaces, 3 above, 3 below

### Why a person decides

The book allows more than one layout and leaves the staccato ambiguity to visual judgment.
