### The rule

Use the compact altered-unison layout with the accidentals before the heads and a normal shared stem. Behind Bars pp. 50–51 permits grouping the accidentals when space is limited. This is the vexml default after comparison with MuseScore, OSMD and alphaTab.

### The test case

Measures 12, 13 and 18 use a flagged dyad, a flagged cluster and a beamed dyad.

### Checks

1. Are the accidentals before the compact flagged dyad?
   Crop: measure 12, 10 spaces, 4 above, 5 below

2. Does the cluster use a normal shared stem?
   Crop: measure 13, 12 spaces, 5 above, 4 below

3. Does the beamed dyad keep the same compact arrangement?
   Crop: measure 18, 12 spaces, 5 above, 4 below
