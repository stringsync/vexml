### The rule

Start a chord’s inner ties after the heads and end them before the next heads; the outer ties may be full length or match. Inner ties cannot reach the heads without running through other heads. See Behind Bars p. 63.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 11. A four-note chord in thirds, F4 A4 C5 E5, tied.

### Renderers
