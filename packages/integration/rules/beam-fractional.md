### The rule

A fractional (partial) beam should be the width of a notehead (Behind Bars p. 17). Matching the notehead width makes short rhythmic hooks easy to spot without crowding neighbouring stems.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 3 holds two dotted-eighth + sixteenth pairs, on G4 (up-stems) and C5 (down-stems), comparing each fractional beam's horizontal length against its notehead width in quarter-spaces.

### What to look for

In measure 3, blue reference lines mark each notehead width (`1.2` spaces) while red brackets across the fractional beams measure `0.9` spaces (`-¼` space short of the notehead width).
