### The rule

A stem joins a diamond notehead at the side of the notehead, not at its central point (Behind Bars p. 11). Side attachment matches modern engraving convention and reserves centred stems for early-music notation.

### The test case

`noteheads.musicxml`: measure 4 on a treble stave in 4/4 with three diamond-headed notes (C5 quarter down-stem, A4 quarter up-stem, and C5 half down-stem), leaving stem placement to vexml.

### What to look for

In measure 4, blue vertical reference lines mark each diamond head's horizontal centre and green boxes highlight where each stem meets its head, labelled `edge`.
