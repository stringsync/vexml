### The rule

A consistent stem direction within a short musical group can override the isolated preference of a chord. (Behind Bars p. 48)

### The test case

`single-stem-chords.musicxml` places a balanced dyad between two chords with high outer extremes in measure 6.

### Checks

1. Does the middle chord maintain the stem direction of the surrounding group?
   Crop: measure 6, 27 spaces

### Why a person decides

The phrase or beat grouping determines whether changing the isolated choice reads better. The book gives editorial latitude.
