### The rule

Place articulation the same distance from each notehead so the eye follows it easily. See Behind Bars p. 119.

### The test case

`articulation.musicxml` isolates each rule by measure.

- Measure 3. Staccato on four notes in spaces, two stem-up and two stem-down.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, a consistent distance from each head
- MuseScore: agrees, dots evenly spaced from the heads
- Verovio: agrees, dots evenly spaced from the heads
- OSMD: agrees, dots evenly spaced from the heads
