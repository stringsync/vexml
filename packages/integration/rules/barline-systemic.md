### The rule

Join the staves at the opening of a system so they read as one score. (Behind Bars p. 38)

### The test case

`barline-score.musicxml` uses two wind parts with aligned staves.

### Checks

1. Does a continuous vertical stroke join the two staves at the opening?
   Crop: measure 1, 5 spaces, 2 above, 10 below
