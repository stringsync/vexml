### The rule

A short beamed group's beam should cross at most one stave-space, and a long group's beam should cross at most two stave-spaces (Behind Bars pp. 19–20). Keeping beam slopes shallow prevents steep beams from cutting across stave lines in a distracting lattice and preserves horizontal rhythmic reading.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 10 holds two wide-interval eighth-note pairs (E4 to E5 and D4 to A5) as short groups, and measure 11 holds an eight-eighth descending run (A5 down to A3) as a long group, measuring each beam's vertical rise or fall in quarter-spaces.

### What to look for

In measure 10, green boxes on the short pairs confirm their slopes stay within 1 space; in measure 11, a red box on the eight-eighth run marks a `6`-space drop where at most `2` spaces is allowed.
