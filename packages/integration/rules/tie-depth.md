### The rule

Bow a shallow tie about 1 to 1½ stave-spaces deep, enough to show through a stave line. A tie too flat disappears into the stave lines. See Behind Bars p. 61.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 1. Two A4 half notes tied.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, about 1 to 1½ spaces deep
- MuseScore: agrees, about a space deep, bowing below the stave
- Verovio: differs, about half a space deep
- OSMD: differs, about half a space deep

### Checks

1. Is the tie about ¾ stave-space deep (½ to 1), counting its stroke?
   Crop: measure 1, 3 above, 4 below

### Why a person decides

Gould calls 1 to 1½ spaces the ideal and states no threshold beyond it, and the renderers draw anything from half a space to a space. The user chose vexml's shallower tie, about ¾ space (decisions.md, 2026-11-26).
