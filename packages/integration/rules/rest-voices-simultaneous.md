### The rule

When both parts on a stave have rests simultaneously, at least one stave line should separate the two rests (Behind Bars p. 36). Clear vertical separation keeps simultaneous rests legible as two distinct voices rather than one merged symbol.

### The test case

`rests-voices.musicxml`: measure 3 opens with a V1 half rest above a simultaneous V2 eighth rest on beat 1, counting how many stave lines fall between the bottom of the upper rest and the top of the lower rest.

### What to look for

In measure 3, a green vertical bracket (`2 lines`) between V1's half rest and V2's eighth rest shows two blue stave lines separating the simultaneous rests.
