### The rule

Under an upper sharp or natural a sixth above, set the lower accidental slightly left so their vertical strokes cannot join; two sharps sit side by side, crossbar edge to crossbar edge, never overlapping. See Behind Bars p. 88.

### The test case

`accidentals.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 12. F#4, D#5 half-note sixth, then E♭4, C#5 half-note sixth.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, the lower sign offset left, sharps edge to edge
- MuseScore: agrees, the lower sign offset left, sharps side by side
- Verovio: agrees, the lower sign offset left, sharps side by side
- OSMD: agrees, the lower sign offset left, sharps side by side
