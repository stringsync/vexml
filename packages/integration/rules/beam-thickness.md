### The rule

Each beam should be ½ stave-space thick, with a ¼ stave-space gap separating adjacent beams (Behind Bars p. 17). Standardizing beam weight and spacing keeps subdivisions legible without filling in or crowding the stave lines.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 1 holds four G4 sixteenth notes (two beams) and measure 2 holds four G4 32nd notes (three beams) in treble clef, measuring every beam band height and inter-beam gap in quarter-spaces.

### What to look for

In measures 1 and 2, green brackets on the left of each beam group read `½` for beam thickness and green brackets on the right read `¼` for the gap between beams.
