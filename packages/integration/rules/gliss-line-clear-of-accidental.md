### The rule

A glissando line does not run through an accidental: it stops short of it or is angled to its left. See Behind Bars p. 141.

### The test case

`glissandos.musicxml` isolates glissando lines by measure.

- Measure 3. `C4` sliding up to `G♯4`, whose sharp stands in the path of the line.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, line stops short of or angles clear of the accidental
- MuseScore: agrees, line ends before the sharp
- Verovio: agrees, line ends before the sharp
- OSMD: differs, line runs into the sharp
