### The rule

Displace adjacent whole heads to the same sides they would occupy with a stem, keeping the second readable. (Behind Bars p. 50)

### The test case

`single-stem-chords.musicxml` uses an A4 and B4 whole-note chord in measure 10.

### Checks

1. Is the lower whole head left of the upper head?
   Crop: measure 10, 8 spaces
