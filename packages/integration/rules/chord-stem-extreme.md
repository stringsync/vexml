### The rule

Use the note farthest from the middle line to set a chord’s stem direction, pointing the stem toward the stave. (Behind Bars pp. 47–48)

### The test case

`single-stem-chords.musicxml` sets the lower extreme farther out in measure 1 and the upper extreme farther out in measure 2, even with three notes.

### Checks

1. Does the dyad stem point up, toward the middle from its low extreme?
   Crop: measure 1, from 7 spaces, 5 spaces

2. Does the three-note chord stem point down from its higher extreme?
   Crop: measure 2, 6 spaces
