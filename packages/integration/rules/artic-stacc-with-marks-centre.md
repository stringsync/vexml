### The rule

When staccato combines with other articulation marks, centre the staccato dot on the notehead like the other marks. See Behind Bars p. 118.

### The test case

`articulation-marks.musicxml` isolates each rule by measure.

- Measure 3. A staccato dot and an accent on each of four notes.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, staccato dot and accent both centred on the notehead
- MuseScore: agrees, dot and accent centred on the notehead
- Verovio: agrees, dot and accent centred on the notehead
- OSMD: agrees, dot and accent centred on the notehead

1. Is each staccato dot centred on its notehead alongside the accent?
