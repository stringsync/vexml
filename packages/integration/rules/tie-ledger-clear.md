### The rule

Keep chord ties off ledger lines, by flattening them or moving them clear of the heads. A tie touching a ledger line reads as part of it. See Behind Bars p. 66.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 26. A chord on ledger lines above, A5 C6 E6, tied.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, ties clear of ledger lines
- MuseScore: agrees, ties clear of the ledger lines
- Verovio: agrees, ties clear of the ledger lines
- OSMD: agrees, ties clear of the ledger lines
