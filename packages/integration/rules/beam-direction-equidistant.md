### The rule

When a beamed group's notes are equidistant from the middle stave line and surrounding context gives no preference, the convention is to use down-stems (Behind Bars pp. 24–25). Defaulting equidistant groups downward matches the single-note middle-line convention.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 22 holds two equidistant eighth-note pairs around middle-line B4 (A4-C5 and G4-D5), with `<stem>` left unset.

### What to look for

In measure 22, the blue line marks the middle stave line; green boxes around both beams read `down`.
