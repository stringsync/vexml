### The rule

Size a standard arc-shaped fermata at about one stave-space high and two stave-spaces wide (about the width of a ledger line). See Behind Bars p. 188.

### The test case

`pauses.musicxml` places a single standard fermata over a B4 whole note in measure 2.

- Measure 2. Whole note B4 with `<fermata>normal</fermata>`.
