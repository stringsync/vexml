### The rule

A stem joins a crossed notehead at the outer edge of the cross rather than at its centre (Behind Bars pp. 11–12). Edge attachment keeps the diagonal crossing legible and aligns crossed notes consistently with standard noteheads.

### The test case

`noteheads.musicxml`: measure 5 on a treble stave in 4/4 with three crossed-head notes (C5 quarter down-stem, A4 quarter up-stem, and C5 half down-stem), leaving stem placement to vexml.

### What to look for

In measure 5, blue vertical reference lines mark each cross head's horizontal centre and green boxes highlight where each stem meets its head, labelled `edge`.
