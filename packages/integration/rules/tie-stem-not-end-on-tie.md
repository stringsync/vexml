### The rule

A stem should not end at the point where it meets a tie; lengthen the stem to cross the tie or shorten it to clear. See Behind Bars p. 68.

### The test case

`ties-voices.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 7. Voice 1 plays beamed eighth notes E4 F4 G4 F4 (up-stems) while voice 2 ties D5 half to D5 half.

### Renderers
