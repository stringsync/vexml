### The rule

For a tuplet with mixed stem directions, place the indication on either side (often above the stave), keeping it clear of slurs, articulations and hairpins. See Behind Bars p. 198.

### Why a person decides

Behind Bars allows either side for mixed-stem tuplets, favouring above the stave when hairpins, slurs or articulations occupy the lower side.

### The test case

`tuplets-placement.musicxml` places a mixed-stem unbeamed quintuplet over a crescendo hairpin in measure 4.

- Measure 4. Unbeamed 5:4 eighth quintuplet (G4, E5, F4, D5, G4) with a crescendo hairpin placed below.
