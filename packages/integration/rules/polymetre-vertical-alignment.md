### The rule

When simultaneous parts carry different time signatures with coinciding barlines, align equivalent beats vertically and place non-equivalent beats in correct rhythmic proportion. See Behind Bars pp. 174–175.

### The test case

`metre-polymetre.musicxml` measure 4 sets Part 1 in 6/4 (six quarters, 3:2 time-modification) against Part 2 in 2/2 (four quarters) with coinciding barlines.

- Measure 4. Beat 1 and the half-bar midpoint (Part 1’s 4th quarter and Part 2’s 3rd quarter) align vertically, while Part 2’s 2nd quarter sits between Part 1’s 2nd and 3rd quarters.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, aligns equivalent beats vertically and spaces non-equivalent beats proportionally
- MuseScore: agrees, aligns beat 1 and the half-bar midpoint vertically across 6/4 and 2/2 and spaces Part 2’s quarters proportionally between Part 1’s quarters
- Verovio: differs, aligns Part 2’s four quarters under Part 1’s first four quarters instead of scaling proportionally across the bar
- OSMD: agrees, aligns beat 1 and the half-bar midpoint vertically and spaces Part 2’s quarters proportionally (though it repeats 6/4 on Part 2)
