### The rule

Use small adjustments between differently directed stems when identical head spacing looks uneven. (Behind Bars p. 41)

### The test case

`rhythmic-spacing.musicxml` uses equal quarter values moving from low, up-stemmed notes to a down-stemmed middle-line note and back.
