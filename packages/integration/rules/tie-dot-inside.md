### The rule

Start a dotted note’s tie at the head, with the dot inside the curve and clear of it. A tie after the dot loses its head; one through the dot hides it. See Behind Bars p. 63.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 14. Dotted quarter A4 tied to an eighth, and dotted quarter C5 tied to an eighth.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, the dot inside the curve
- MuseScore: agrees, the tie starts at the head and bows clear of the dot
- Verovio: agrees, the tie starts at the head and bows clear of the dot
- OSMD: agrees, the tie starts at the head and bows clear of the dot

### Checks

1. Do both ties start at their heads and pass clear of the dots?
