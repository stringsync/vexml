### The rule

When a semibreve rest represents the sum of four crotchet rests within a larger bar (such as half a bar of 4/2), place it at the start of its duration rather than centring it in the bar. See Behind Bars p. 160.

### The test case

`metre-whole-rests.musicxml` measure 9 is in 4/2 with a semibreve rest on beats 1–2 followed by two half notes on beats 3–4.

- Measure 9. Half-bar semibreve rest followed by two half notes in 4/2.

- OSMD: agrees, places the semibreve rest at beat 1 before the two half notes
