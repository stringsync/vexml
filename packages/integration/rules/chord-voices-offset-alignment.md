### The rule

Align an accompanying part with the left-hand head of an offset unison or second, including either permitted whole-note ordering. (Behind Bars p. 53)

### The test case

`double-stem-chords.musicxml` places a clear reference onset in the lower stave under unlike unisons and an adjacent pair.
