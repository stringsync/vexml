### The rule

Replace the ordinary divider at a repeat with an oriented pair of thin and thick strokes and repeat dots. (Behind Bars p. 39)

### The test case

`barlines.musicxml` uses forward and backward repeat signs around the same measure.

### Checks

1. Does the forward sign put the dots to the right of its two strokes?
   Crop: measure 4, 4 spaces

2. Does the backward sign put the dots to the left of its two strokes?
   Crop: measure 4, from 10 spaces, 5 spaces

3. Is the heavy stroke as thick as a beam, with a clear gap of about one-third of a stave space?
   Crop: measure 4, 5 spaces

4. Are both thin strokes as heavy and as solid as an ordinary barline?
   Crop: measure 4, 5 spaces
