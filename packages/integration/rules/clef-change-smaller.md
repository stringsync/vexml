### The rule

A clef change placed after the beginning of a system is drawn at about two-thirds the size of the opening clef (Behind Bars p. 7). Reducing mid-system clefs keeps them visually distinct from system openings and less disruptive to note spacing.

### The test case

`clefs.musicxml`: a five-line stave in 4/4 that opens with a full-size treble clef in measure 1 and changes back to treble clef after beat 2 of measure 4.

### What to look for

A blue reference bracket beside measure 1's opening treble clef shows its full height in stave-spaces. A measurement bracket beside measure 4's mid-bar treble clef shows its height as a ratio of the opening clef, which should fall between `0.6×` and `0.75×`.
