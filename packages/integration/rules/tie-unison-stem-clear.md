### The rule

On a tied single-stemmed unison, the stem must not intersect a tie. See Behind Bars p. 69.

### The test case

`ties-voices.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 9. Single-stemmed half-note perfect unison on G4, both heads tied.

### Renderers

- MuseScore: agrees, both unison ties stay clear of the up-stem
