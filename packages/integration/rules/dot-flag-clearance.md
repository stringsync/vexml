# dot-flag-clearance

### The rule

Keep an up-stem flag clear of the duration dot by moving the dot past the flag or lengthening the stem. See Behind Bars p. 55.

### The test case

`duration-dots.musicxml` uses one attack per measure to isolate the dot placement.

- Measure 5. Up-stemmed flagged note on a line.

### Checks

1. Is the dot clear of the flag?
   Crop: measure 5, 7 spaces, 6 above, 4 below
