### The rule

Eighth and shorter rests place each hook in a separate stave space: eighth and sixteenth rests begin in the second space down, while 32nd and 64th rests begin in the top space (Behind Bars p. 35). Keeping every hook inside a space prevents hooks from disappearing into stave lines.

### The test case

`rests.musicxml`: measure 5 sets eighth, sixteenth, 32nd, and 64th rests in sequence, checking which stave space each rest's top edge lands in.

### What to look for

In measure 5, four green boxes label the starting space of the eighth (`sp 2`), sixteenth (`sp 2`), 32nd (`sp 1`), and 64th (`sp 1`) rests.
