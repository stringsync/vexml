### The rule

Keep a tie clear of articulation on its notes, bringing its ends to the heads’ edges or just inside them. A tie through an accent or tenuto obscures both. See Behind Bars p. 62.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 9. A4 quarters tied, the first with an accent below and the second with a tenuto below.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, the tie clear of articulation
- MuseScore: agrees, moves the accent below the tie
- Verovio: agrees, moves the accent below and runs the tie between the heads
- OSMD: differs, the tie starts against the accent

### Checks

1. Is the tie clear of the accent and the tenuto?
   Crop: measure 9, 8 spaces, 3 above, 5 below
