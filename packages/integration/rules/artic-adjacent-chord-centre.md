### The rule

On a chord of adjacent notes, centre articulation on the notehead on the correct side of the stem. See Behind Bars p. 119.

### The test case

`articulation.musicxml` isolates each rule by measure.

- Measure 11. Staccato on two chords of adjacent notes.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, centred on the correct-side head
- MuseScore: agrees, dot over the correct-side head
- Verovio: agrees, dot over the correct-side head
- OSMD: agrees, dot over the correct-side head

### Checks

1. Is each dot centred on the notehead on the correct side of the stem?
   Crop: measure 11, 4 above, 6 below

### Why a person decides

Gould says which head to centre on but not how far from the stem a dot may sit.
