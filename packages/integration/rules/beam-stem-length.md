### The rule

With one or two beams, the stem length from notehead centre to outer beam edge should be 3½ stave-spaces for notes in a space and shortened to 3¼ stave-spaces for notes on a line (Behind Bars pp. 18–19). Shortening line-note stems by ¼ space keeps an added inner beam aligned to a stave line instead of mid-space.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measures 4–7 hold repeated eighths (one beam) and sixteenths (two beams) on space notes (A4 in M4, C5 in M6) and line notes (G4 in M5, D5 in M7), measuring every stem from notehead centre to the outer beam edge.

### What to look for

In measures 4–7, brackets beside each one- and two-beam stem report its length in spaces: the eighth-note space pairs in M4 and M6 pass in green at `3½`, while line-note eighths in M5 and M7 stay `3½` (expected `3¼`) and all sixteenth-note stems stretch to `4¼`, marked in red.
