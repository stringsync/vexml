### The rule

Keep the slur centred on the stems at its ends when staccato dots sit at the stem ends. See Behind Bars p. 122.

### The test case

`slurs-structure.musicxml` isolates each rule by measure.

- Measure 6. A slur above four stems-up notes whose staccato dots sit above the stems.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, centres the slur on the stems over the dots
- MuseScore: agrees, ends the slur over the first and last stems, over the dots
- Verovio: agrees, ends the slur over the first and last stems, over the dots
- OSMD: agrees, ends the slur over the first and last stems, over the dots
