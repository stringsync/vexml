### The rule

Run a tie from head to head, almost touching each. An arc that stands off from the heads, or ends at a stem, reads as a slur. See Behind Bars pp. 60–61.

### The test case

`ties.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 1. Two A4 half notes tied.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, ends almost touching the heads
- MuseScore: agrees, ends just under the heads
- Verovio: agrees, ends beside the heads
- OSMD: agrees, ends beside the heads

### Checks

1. Do both ends of the tie almost touch their heads?
   Crop: measure 1, 3 above, 4 below
