### The rule

When a stem is shortened below 3 stave-spaces, its tail must not overshoot past the notehead (Behind Bars p. 16). Avoiding overshoot keeps flagged outward notes legible in two-voice passages.

### The test case

`tails.musicxml`: measure 2 places voice 1 A5 and C6 eighth notes above a voice 2 C4 whole note so both eighth notes take outward up-stems.

### What to look for

In measure 2, blue lines mark the bottom of the A5 and C6 noteheads; green boxes around both tails read `beside the head`, showing neither tail dips below its notehead.
