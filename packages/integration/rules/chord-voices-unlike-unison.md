### The rule

Give unlike head types distinct positions at a unison, normally displacing the lower voice right. (Behind Bars pp. 52–53)

### The test case

`double-stem-chords.musicxml` pairs half with quarter, whole with half, and quarter with whole.

### Checks

1. Does the lower quarter sit right of the half note?
   Crop: measure 7, 7 spaces

2. Do the whole and half each have a distinct head?
   Crop: measure 8, 7 spaces

3. Do the quarter and whole each have a distinct head?
   Crop: measure 9, from 3 spaces, 7 spaces
