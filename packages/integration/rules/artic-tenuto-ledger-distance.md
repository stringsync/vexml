### The rule

Keep tenuto lines further from ledger lines than other marks, so the eye does not confuse the two. See Behind Bars p. 121.

### The test case

`articulation-marks.musicxml` isolates each rule by measure.

- Measure 4. A tenuto line on each of four notes below the stave.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, tenuto clear of the ledger lines
- MuseScore: agrees, tenuto clear of the ledger notes
- Verovio: agrees, tenuto clear of the ledger notes
- OSMD: agrees, tenuto clear of the ledger notes
