### The rule

In two-voice writing, stems pointing away from the stave shorten progressively as notes move outward (from 3½ to 3 to 2½ stave-spaces) and never become shorter than 2½ spaces (Behind Bars pp. 14–15). Progressive shortening prevents outward stems from pushing systems apart unnecessarily.

### The test case

`stems.musicxml`: measure 8 pairs voice 1 rising outward (F5, A5, C6, E6 quarters, forced up-stem by the second voice) over a voice 2 C4 whole note, with `<stem>` unset.

### What to look for

In measure 8, brackets beside the four voice-1 stems show each length in quarter stave-spaces: F5 stays `3½`, A5 shortens to `3`, and C6 and E6 shorten to `2½` (never shorter).
