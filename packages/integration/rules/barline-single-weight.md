### The rule

Make the ordinary barline heavier than the stave lines and stems so it stays readable among notes. (Behind Bars p. 38)

### The test case

`barlines.musicxml` uses a regular divider between two measures of stemmed notes.

### Checks

1. Is the first divider heavier than both the stave lines and the adjacent stems?
   Crop: measure 1, from 23 spaces, 8 spaces
