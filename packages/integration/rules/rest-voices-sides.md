### The rule

In two-part writing on a single stave, upper-part rests are placed above the middle stave line and lower-part rests below it (Behind Bars p. 36). Keeping each voice's rests on its own side makes it immediately clear which part is silent.

### The test case

`rests-voices.musicxml`: measure 1 sets two voices in 4/4 treble clef (V1 has a quarter rest; V2 has a quarter rest and a half rest), checking whether each rest's centre lies above or below the middle line.

### What to look for

In measure 1, green boxes around V1's quarter rest (`V1 above`) and V2's quarter and half rests (`V2 below`) confirm each rest sits on its voice's side of the blue middle stave line.
