### The rule

Grace notes take shorter, lighter ledger lines than full-sized notes, scaled in proportion to their smaller noteheads (Behind Bars p. 26). Proportional ledgers keep ornamental notes visually subordinate to the main beats they precede.

### The test case

`ledgers.musicxml`: measure 3 pairs a slashed eighth grace note with a full-sized quarter note at the same pitch, first above the stave (C6) and then below it (A3), comparing each grace note's ledger width with its main note's ledger width.

### What to look for

In measure 3, green brackets on the grace C6 and grace A3 ledgers report `12px`, shorter than the blue reference brackets on the following full-sized C6 and A3 ledgers (`18px`).
