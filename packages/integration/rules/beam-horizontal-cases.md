### The rule

A beam should be horizontal when the group begins and ends on the same pitch, when it forms a repeated pitch pattern, or when an inner note lies closer to the beam than either outer note (a concave shape) (Behind Bars pp. 22–23). Flattening these shapes prevents awkward tilts that would crowd the closest inner note or misrepresent a level contour.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 16 holds a same-ended group (C5, G5, E5, C5) and a repeated-pattern group (E4, G4, E4, G4), and measure 17's first group holds a concave shape (G4, C5, A4 with up-stems), checking that all three beams are flat.

### What to look for

In measure 16 and the first group of measure 17, green boxes on all three beams confirm they are drawn `flat` (0-space slope).
