# dot-unison-pronged

### The rule

Prefer a dot beside each head of a pronged chord and allow a shared column when space is limited. See Behind Bars p. 58.

### The test case

`duration-dots.musicxml` uses one attack per measure to isolate the dot placement.

- Measure 23. Altered unison on one stem.

### Checks

1. Do the dots read with their own heads in this altered unison?
   Crop: measure 23, 9 spaces, 6 above, 4 below

### Why a person decides

The compact alternative is permitted and the choice depends on visual attribution. The altered-unison stem work is tracked by #4071.
