### The rule

When a beamed group has equal numbers of notes above and below the middle stave line, the note furthest from the middle line determines the group's stem direction (Behind Bars p. 24). Following the furthest note keeps stems inside the stave instead of protruding far outside it.

### The test case

`beams.musicxml`: measure 20 holds two four-note eighth groups with two notes on each side of the middle line (G5-E4-F4-F5, furthest G5 above; and D5-D4-E4-C5, furthest D4 below), with `<stem>` left unset.

### What to look for

In measure 20, the blue line marks the middle stave line; green boxes around the two beams read `down` for the first group and `up` for the second.
