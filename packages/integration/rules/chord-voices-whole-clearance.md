### The rule

Keep a whole head clear of the other voice’s stem so it cannot be read as a stemmed duration. (Behind Bars p. 53)

### The test case

`double-stem-chords.musicxml` pairs whole heads with stemmed heads at unison in measures 8 and 9 and at a second in measure 10.

### Checks

1. Is the whole head clear of the half note’s stem?
   Crop: measure 8, 7 spaces

2. Is the whole head clear of the quarter note’s stem?
   Crop: measure 9, from 3 spaces, 7 spaces

3. Does the adjacent whole head stay clear of the lower stem?
   Crop: measure 10, 7 spaces
