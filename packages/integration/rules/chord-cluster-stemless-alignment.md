### The rule

Align a simultaneous part with the first head of a stemless altered cluster or with the implied stem position of an adjacent-note chord. (Behind Bars p. 52)

### The test case

`double-stem-chords.musicxml` places a reference whole note under a horizontally arranged altered cluster.

### Checks

1. Does the lower whole note align with the first head of the upper cluster?
   Crop: measure 13, part 1, from 4 spaces, 13 spaces, 3 above, 12 below
