### The rule

When overlapping parts are not tied simultaneously, curve each part’s tie towards its own stem as normal. See Behind Bars p. 67.

### The test case

`ties-voices.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 3. Voice 1 ties G4 half to G4 half (up-stem) while voice 2 moves C5 B4 C5 above it (down-stem).

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, G4 tie curves up towards its stem
- MuseScore: agrees, G4 tie curves up towards its stem
- Verovio: agrees, G4 tie curves up towards its stem
- OSMD: agrees, G4 tie curves up towards its stem

### Checks

1. Does the G4 tie in voice 1 curve up towards its stem?
   Crop: measure 3, 4 above, 4 below
