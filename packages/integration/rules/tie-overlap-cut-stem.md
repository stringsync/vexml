### The rule

Keep a tie close to its noteheads and let it cut through the other part’s stem rather than curving a long way from the notehead like a slur. See Behind Bars p. 68.

### The test case

`ties-voices.musicxml` gives each case its own measure.

- Measure 6. Voice 1 ties G4 half to G4 half while voice 2 plays D5 C5 D5 with down-stems crossing the tie.
