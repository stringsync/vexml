### The rule

Notes sitting on more than one ledger line extend their stems all the way to the middle stave line (Behind Bars p. 14). Reaching the centre line anchors distant ledger notes visually back to the stave.

### The test case

`stems.musicxml`: measure 5 holds C6, E6, A3, and F3 quarter notes (each on two or three ledger lines above or below the treble stave) with `<stem>` left unset.

### What to look for

In measure 5, green brackets from each notehead centre to its stem tip meet the blue middle-line reference and read `0` spaces from the middle line.
