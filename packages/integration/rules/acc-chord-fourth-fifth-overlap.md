### The rule

Flats and naturals a fourth or fifth apart may overlap horizontally, saving space, as long as their strokes do not join. See Behind Bars p. 88.

### The test case

`accidentals.musicxml` gives each case its own measure. The user chose to tuck two flats a fifth apart (~0.56 spaces) and keep two naturals a fourth apart clear of each other after reviewing the strip.

- Measure 14. E♭4, B♭4 half-note fifth, then F♮4, B♮4 half-note fourth.

### Renderers

- Behind Bars: agrees, flats and naturals tucked partly under each other
- MuseScore: differs, aligns the two flats and offsets the naturals slightly
- Verovio: differs, aligns the two flats and offsets the naturals slightly
- OSMD: agrees, both lower signs tucked partly under the upper ones

### Why a person decides

Gould permits the overlap rather than requiring it, and a full offset is the other acceptable reading.
