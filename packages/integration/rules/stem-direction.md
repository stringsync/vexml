### The rule

Notes placed above the middle stave line take down-stems and notes below the middle stave line take up-stems (Behind Bars p. 13). Pointing stems inward toward the centre of the stave keeps vertical extent compact and avoids colliding with adjacent staves.

### The test case

`stems.musicxml`: measure 1 holds C5, D5, E5, and F5 above the middle line, and measure 2 holds A4, G4, E4, and C4 below it, all quarter notes in treble clef with `<stem>` left unset.

### What to look for

Blue horizontal reference marks the middle stave line; green brackets beside each stem in measure 1 read `down` and in measure 2 read `up`.
